- **NPR News** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **BBC News** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **Reuters Top News** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **ProPublica** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **The Moscow Times** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **Meduza** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **WIRED Top Stories** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **TechCrunch Startups** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **Hacker News (Show HN)** — works via hnrss.org, has title/link/date/description.
- **arXiv cs.AI** — works, Atom format, has title/link/published date/summary.
- **ScienceDaily** — works, has title/link/date/description.
- **MIT News Research** — works, has title/link/date/description.

## Rejected sources

- **AP News** — RSS feed returned 404.
- **Politico** — robots.txt returns 403.
- **WIPO PATENTSCOPE** — connection errors / HTML instead of XML.
- **USPTO PatFT** — no standard RSS feed available.
- **EurekAlert** — bot blocking / HTML instead of XML.
- **CORDIS** — connection errors.

## Fast source candidates

- **NPR News** — high-frequency updates, major US outlet.
- **BBC News** — high-frequency updates, global reach.
- **Reuters Top News** — fastest wire news, global scope.
- **Meduza** — fast Russian-language feed.

## Opportunity source candidates

- **arXiv cs.AI / cs.LG** — daily preprints, AI and ML research.
- **ScienceDaily** — multi-topic science aggregator.
- **MIT News Research** — institutional research announcements.
- **Phys.org** — science news aggregator (not yet in seed, add when needed).
- **Nature News** — high-impact scientific discoveries (not yet in seed, add when needed).
- **WIRED Science** — tech and science cross-over (not yet in seed, add when needed).
- **TechCrunch Startups** — startup funding and product launches.
- **Hacker News (Show HN)** — community-discovered projects and tools.
